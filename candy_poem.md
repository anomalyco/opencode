# Candy Dreams

In wrappers bright and colors gay,
Sweet confections dance and play.
Gummy bears with bounces light,
Chocolate kisses, pure delight.

Lollipops in rainbow swirls,
Candy that makes tongues do twirls.
Jelly beans in jars so neat,
Each one a different flavor treat.

Caramel chews with golden glow,
Marshmallows soft as winter snow.
Licorice ropes in black and red,
Sugar crystals, enough said.

The candy store, a wonderland,
Where sweetness reigns at your command.
A childhood joy that never fades,
In candy dreams, the magic wades.

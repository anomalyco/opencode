# Export + QA checklist

## Content checks

- Hook lands in first 2 seconds (clear promise + visual proof)
- Only 3 differentiators max; each is demoed
- CTA is singular and repeated (end card + spoken)
- No jargon without an on-screen explanation

## UI recording checks

- No personal data, API keys, private repos, or DMs on screen
- Fonts are readable at 100% on a phone
- Cursor is visible; clicks are readable
- Avoid long scrolls; prefer jump cuts

## Audio checks

- VO is intelligible over music (duck music under VO)
- Captions match VO; line breaks are natural
- Sound effects are subtle and consistent

## Export defaults

- 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264 (high profile), 15–30 Mbps
- Social cutdowns: 1080×1080 and 1080×1920
- End card: 2–3 seconds, static, high contrast

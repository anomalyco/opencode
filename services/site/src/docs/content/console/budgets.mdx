---
title: "Budgets"
description: "Cap monthly spend for a workspace, its members, and its service accounts."
---

Budgets cap monthly spend through policies. Owners and admins manage them in **Controls**.

## Budget levels

| Policy audience        | Applies to                                           |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Entire workspace       | Combined spend of all members and service accounts   |
| All members            | Each member, or one shared allowance for all members |
| Members tagged with    | Members with the chosen tags                         |
| These members          | Specific members                                     |
| All service accounts   | Each service account, or one shared allowance        |
| These service accounts | Specific service accounts                            |

A request must fit under every applicable budget. A larger allowance never overrides a smaller one. Without any
budget, spend is unlimited.

## Workspace budget

The workspace budget blocks requests for everyone once the workspace reaches it.

1. Open **Controls** and click **Add policy**.
2. Choose **Entire workspace** and add a **Budget** rule.
3. Enter the monthly limit in USD and click **Save**.

## Member budgets

Choose **All members** in a policy to give each member a monthly allowance, or share one amount across the group.
Choose specific members or tags to cover a smaller group.

To budget for one person:

1. Open the member in **Settings › Members**.
2. Click **Create budget policy** to open the builder with that person selected.
3. Enter the amount and click **Save**.

Their member page lists the policies that apply. If an all-member budget also covers them, both limits apply.

## Service account budgets

In **Controls**, choose **All service accounts** or **These service accounts**, add a **Budget** rule, and set an
individual or shared monthly allowance. The policy covers every key belonging to those accounts.

## Amounts and resets

- Amounts are in US dollars with up to two decimal places.
- Budgets reset on the 1st of each month at 00:00 UTC.
- A `$0` budget blocks all spend that counts toward budgets.
- Adding or changing a budget includes spend already recorded that month; it does not reset the allowance.

## What counts

By default, a budget covers **All spend**. You can narrow it to selected models; then only those models count,
with either one allowance for the set or a separate allowance for each model.

| Spend                                                               | Counts toward budgets        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Paid Console models                                                 | Yes                          |
| Models on [your own providers](/console/providers) with a price set | Yes, at the configured price |
| [Web search](/console/websearch)                                    | Yes, for all-spend budgets   |
| Free models                                                         | No                           |
| [Go](/console/go) usage within the plan                             | No                           |
| Go extra usage paid from credits                                    | Yes                          |

## When a budget is reached

New requests fail with HTTP `429` until the budget resets or an owner or admin raises it. The response identifies
a policy budget failure but does not name the budget. Open the request in **Activity › Logs** to see the policy
that blocked it.

```text
Upstream request failed: Policy budget exceeded
```

Budgets are checked when a request starts. Requests already in progress finish, so spend can end slightly above the
limit.

## Track spend

Each member sees their own spending and limit on **Activity**. Open **My usage** for **Activity › Overview**,
including budget details and reset dates.

Owners and admins can open **Activity › Usage** from the spending chart. It breaks down cost by day, model, and
person, and the **Export usage** button downloads daily totals for the last 7, 30, or 90 days as CSV.

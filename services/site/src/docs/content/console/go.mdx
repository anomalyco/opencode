OpenCode Go gives you reliable access to popular open coding models, with three monthly plans:

| Plan         | Price                                  | Included usage                                                             |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Go**       | **$10/month**                          | Lower-cost access to the models below                                      |
| **Go Plus**  | **$40/month**                          | Higher usage limits across the models below                                |
| **Go Teams** | **$20/member/month**, 2-member minimum | Every member gets their own limits, $5/month higher than Go on every model |

Go works like any other provider in OpenCode. You subscribe to OpenCode Go and get your API key. It's **completely optional** and you don't need it to use OpenCode.

The service is designed primarily for international users and provides stable global access.

## How it works

1. Sign in to the [OpenCode Console](https://opencode.ai/console), subscribe to Go, Go Plus, or Go Teams, add your billing details, and copy your API key.
2. Run `/connect` in the TUI, select **OpenCode Go**, and paste your API key.

   ```text
   /connect
   ```

3. Run `/models` to select a model available through Go.

   ```text
   /models
   ```

<Callout>
  Go and Go Plus cover the member who subscribes and the workspace's API keys. Other members you invite use free models
  or the workspace balance. Either can upgrade to Go Teams at any time, with the unused part of the month's allowance
  credited. Go Teams covers every member of the workspace at $20 per member per month, with a 2-member minimum
  ($40/month). The second seat covers the next member who joins at no extra charge; after that, a member who joins
  mid-period is charged $20 and gets a full monthly allowance. Service accounts are not covered by Go Teams.
</Callout>

See the [current list of models](https://opencode.ai/console/go#models). It may change as we test and add new ones.

### Enable restricted models

Workspace privacy settings apply to Go. By default, paid endpoints that train on request data are blocked, and
allowed regions are **Europe & United States**. Workspaces without saved privacy settings also allow **Global**
for requests from China.

In **Settings › Privacy**, an owner or admin can enable:

- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and V4 Pro:** select **Global** under **Regions**.
- **LongCat 2.5:** select **Global** under **Regions**.
- **Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor and Muse Spark 1.2 Contributor:** turn on **Allow paid endpoints that train on request data**.

See [Privacy](#privacy) for each model's training and retention terms.

## Pay with credit

Owners and admins can pay for Go, including renewals and upgrades, from the workspace's credit. Choose **Credit** as
the payment method when you subscribe, or later in **Manage subscription**. This is separate from
[Extra Usage](#usage-beyond-limits), which pays for usage past your limits.

- The full price comes from your credit balance. If the balance is short, you can add credit first.
- Renewals take the price from your credit when the period ends. If there isn't enough, Go stays active while the
  renewal retries 1, 3, and 7 days after the period ends, and again as soon as enough credit is added. If the last
  retry fails, Go ends.
- Workspaces on [invoiced billing](/console/billing#invoiced-billing) can't pay for Go with credit.
- A refund of a Go payment made with credit goes back to your credit and ends that Go period.

## Where can I use it?

OpenCode Go is designed for [OpenCode](https://opencode.ai) and other coding agents
that produce similar types of requests. Traffic is monitored for abuse that
degrades the experience for other users.

Your client should:

1. Send typical coding agent traffic
2. Identify itself with its own user agent, such as `my-coding-agent/1.0`, rather
   than a generic SDK or HTTP-library name.
3. Send a stable session ID in `x-opencode-session` for each conversation so we can optimize routing and
   prompt caching.

[Decision](#decision-models) and [embedding](#embedding-models) requests don't need `x-opencode-session`, so any
client, such as a RAG library, a memory provider or a script, can call them with your Go key.

See [Harnesses](/console/harnesses) to set up Go in Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw, Pi, and Raycast.

## Usage limits

The paid models you use on Go share one allowance. On Go Teams, each member has their own allowance across models.

- The **5-hour limit** is 20% of your monthly allowance. The window starts when you first use your allowance and lasts five hours.
- The **weekly limit** is 50% of your monthly allowance. It resets on Monday at 00:00 UTC.
- Your **monthly allowance** resets when a new paid period starts.

Switching models draws from the same allowance. If you reach a limit, you can use a free model or turn on
[Extra Usage](#usage-beyond-limits) to use your Console credits until you have allowance available again.

Each model's [monthly limit](https://opencode.ai/console/go#limits) shows how much usage your plan covers if you use only that model over the month.
For example, $1.50 of usage on a model with a $15 limit uses 10% of your monthly allowance. So does $6 on a model
with a $60 limit. Go responses report how much each request counted in [`opencode.allowance`](/console/api/inference#cost).

[Token prices](https://opencode.ai/console/go#pricing) are per 1M tokens and are the same for Go, Go Plus, and Go Teams.

[Decision](#decision-models) and [embedding](#embedding-models) models come at list price, with no Go discount: their
monthly limit matches what you pay, $10 on Go and $40 on Go Plus. On Go Teams it's $15, $5 higher than Go as on every
model.

**Beam Free:** Free for a limited time. Not available in Angola, Belarus, Cambodia, China (including Hong Kong and Macau), Cuba, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Iran, Iraq, Laos, Libya, Nigeria, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Venezuela, or the Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions of Ukraine.

**Gemini 4 Argon:** The 50% discount applies through November 30, 2026.

**HungerG Free:** Free for a limited time.

### Usage beyond limits

If you also have credits in your Console balance, you can turn on **Extra Usage** on the
**Go** page. When enabled, Go will fall back to your [pay-as-you-go balance](/console/models#pricing)
after you've reached your usage limits instead of blocking requests.

Go Teams starts with **Extra Usage** on, and you can turn it off or on again on the **Go** page.

### Why some models have lower usage

With Go, the included monthly usage varies by model.

For most models, we make this work through bulk discounts and reserved GPU capacity. We then pass those savings on to you as higher monthly usage.

For some models, we haven't had the opportunity to negotiate a discount or host them at a lower cost, either because the model is new or because their public pricing is already discounted.

For these models, you still get a little more than if you paid the model providers directly; this is why their included monthly usage is lower.

## Endpoints

You can also access Go models through the API at `https://opencode.ai/inference/go`. See each model's
[ID, path and AI SDK package](https://opencode.ai/console/go#api). Each model is listed with its native API, but every
text model also works through the other APIs, which Console translates. See
[Base URLs](/console/harnesses#base-urls) for the full set.

These AI SDK packages are for applications calling Go directly. To use Go in OpenCode, connect with `/connect` and set
the [model](/models) using the format `opencode-go/<model-id>`:

```jsonc title="opencode.jsonc"
{
  "$schema": "https://opencode.ai/config.json",
  "model": "opencode-go/kimi-k3",
}
```

### Models

Fetch the full list of available models and their metadata from the models endpoint:

```bash
curl https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/models
```

See the [model list API](/console/api/models) for every field in the response, including Go's monthly usage limits.

### Decision models

Decision models answer typed questions about a `state` instead of generating text. Send the same System One request
as on [Console models](/console/models#jev) to `https://opencode.ai/inference/go/systemone/v1/systemone` with your Go
key:

```bash
curl https://opencode.ai/inference/go/systemone/v1/systemone \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENCODE_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "jev-1.14",
    "state": "My payments have failed for three days and I am losing sales.",
    "questions": {
      "is_urgent": {
        "type": "noul",
        "instructions": "Does this request require urgent attention?"
      }
    }
  }'
```

| Model                               | Model ID            | Input  | Output | Cached Read | Cached Write |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------ | ------ | ----------- | ------------ |
| Jev 1.14                            | jev-1.14            | $0.042 | Free   | -           | -            |
| Gemini Decision 1                   | gemini-decision-1   | $0.10  | Free   | -           | -            |
| GPT 6 Luna Decision (≤ 272K tokens) | gpt-6-luna-decision | $0.10  | Free   | $0.01       | $0.125       |
| GPT 6 Luna Decision (> 272K tokens) | gpt-6-luna-decision | $0.20  | Free   | $0.02       | $0.25        |

- Prices are per 1M tokens.
- Requests in OpenAI's Decisions format work at `https://opencode.ai/inference/go/openai/v1/decisions`.
- See [Jev](/console/models#jev) for question types, and
  [GPT 6 Luna Decision and Gemini Decision 1](/console/models#gpt-6-luna-decision-and-gemini-decision-1) for images,
  audio, video, and PDFs in `state`.

### Embedding models

Embedding models turn text into vectors. Call them on OpenAI's Embeddings API at
`https://opencode.ai/inference/go/openai/v1/embeddings` with your Go key:

```bash
curl https://opencode.ai/inference/go/openai/v1/embeddings \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENCODE_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "text-embedding-3-small",
    "input": ["hello world", "goodbye"]
  }'
```

| Model                  | Model ID               | Input |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ----- |
| text-embedding-3-small | text-embedding-3-small | $0.02 |
| Qwen3 Embedding 8B     | qwen3-embedding-8b     | $0.01 |
| Gemini Embedding 2     | gemini-embedding-2     | $0.20 |

- Prices are per 1M input tokens.
- The request also takes `dimensions` and `encoding_format`. See [Embedding models](/console/models#embedding-models).

### Usage

Check how much of your [usage limits](#usage-limits) you've used, for example to show them in your own client:

```bash
curl https://opencode.ai/inference/go/v1/usage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>"
```

Use the same key you use for Go. On Go and Go Plus, that is the subscriber's
[personal API key](/console/api#personal-api-keys) or a [service account key](/console/api#service-accounts). On Go
Teams, it is any member's personal API key.

The response has one meter for each limit: `rolling` for the 5-hour limit, `weekly`, and `monthly`.

```json
{
  "usage": {
    "rolling": { "status": "ok", "percent": 30, "resetsAt": "2026-11-21T15:00:00.000Z" },
    "weekly": { "status": "rate-limited", "percent": 100, "resetsAt": "2026-11-23T00:00:00.000Z" },
    "monthly": { "status": "ok", "percent": 50, "resetsAt": "2026-12-01T00:00:00.000Z" }
  }
}
```

| Field      | Meaning                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `status`   | `ok`, or `rate-limited` once the limit is used up.                                                         |
| `percent`  | How much of the limit is used, as a whole number from `0` to `100`.                                        |
| `resetsAt` | When the limit resets, in ISO 8601. Before the 5-hour window starts, `rolling` resets five hours from now. |

Requests without a valid key return HTTP `401`. Keys Go doesn't cover return HTTP `403` with a message that explains
why, for example when the workspace has no active Go subscription.

```json
{ "type": "error", "error": { "type": "EntitlementError", "message": "OpenCode Go subscription required." } }
```

## Background

Open models have gotten really good. They now reach performance close to proprietary models for coding tasks. Because many providers can serve them competitively, they are usually far cheaper.

However, getting reliable, low latency access to them can be difficult. Providers vary in quality and availability.

<Callout type="tip">We tested a select group of models and providers that work well with OpenCode.</Callout>

To fix this, we did a couple of things:

1. We tested a select group of open models and talked to their teams about how to best run them.
2. We worked with a few providers to make sure these were being served correctly.
3. We benchmarked the combination of the model/provider and came up with a list that we feel good recommending.

Go, Go Plus, and Go Teams all provide access to these models; choose the plan that fits how much you use them, or Go
Teams to cover your whole team.

## Goals

We created OpenCode Go to:

1. Make AI coding **accessible** to more people with a low cost subscription.
2. Provide **reliable** access to the best open coding models.
3. Curate models that are **tested and benchmarked** for coding agent use.
4. Have **no lock-in** by allowing you to use any other provider with OpenCode as well.

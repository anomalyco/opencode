---
title: "Sign in and your account"
description: "How Console sign-in methods relate to your account email, and how to add a password to a Google or GitHub account."
---

Your Console account is the email address you signed up with. You can sign in with email and password, Google, or
GitHub.

## Your account email

A Google or GitHub sign-up uses that provider's email address as your Console account email:

- **Google** uses the account's verified email address.
- **GitHub** uses the account's verified primary email address.

If you sign in with Google or GitHub using an email that already has a Console account, you're signed into that
account. Console adds the provider to it rather than creating a second account.

## Add a password to a Google or GitHub account

An account created with Google or GitHub has no password. To set one:

1. Go to the [Console](https://opencode.ai/console) sign-in page and click **Forgot?** beside the password field.
2. Enter your account email and click **Send reset instructions**.
3. Open the emailed link, choose a new password, then sign in with email and password.

Passwords need at least 10 characters and one uppercase letter, number, or symbol. Signing in with Google or GitHub
keeps working.

## One email, one account

Console treats each email address as one account. Two addresses are the same account only when they differ by
capitalisation alone.

- `alex@company.com` and `Alex@company.com` are the same account.
- `alex.smith@gmail.com` and `alexsmith@gmail.com` are different accounts.
- `alex+cursor@gmail.com` and `alex@gmail.com` are different accounts.

Dots and `+` tags in Gmail addresses are part of the address as far as Console is concerned. If you signed up with one
form and later sign in with another, you get a separate account without your existing workspaces, keys, or billing
history.

Console doesn't merge accounts, and your account email can't be changed in **Settings › Account**.

Send a message to another agent in your team.

This enables inter-agent communication within a team:
- **Member → Lead**: Send findings, status updates, or responses to the team lead
- **Lead → Member**: Route challenges, requests for elaboration, or cross-review findings to specific agents

Usage:
- You must be part of a team to use this tool
- Specify the team_id and either a specific agent name or "lead" as the recipient
- The message content should be clear and actionable
- Messages are delivered asynchronously — the recipient processes them on their next turn

When receiving messages, respond substantively. For cross-review challenges, provide evidence from the spec or codebase.

Send a direct message to another agent in your team, or broadcast to all teammates.

This tool allows teammates in an Agent Team to communicate directly with each other, bypassing the lead agent. This is useful when:
- One teammate finishes defining an interface/type that another teammate needs
- You need to coordinate which files to avoid editing simultaneously
- You want to share intermediate results or discoveries with a specific teammate

You must specify the team_id and the target session_id (or "*" for broadcast).
